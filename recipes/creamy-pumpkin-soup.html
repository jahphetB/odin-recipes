Creamy Pumpkin Soup

Description

Pumpkin soup is a warm, smooth, and comforting meal.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups chopped pumpkin
  • 3 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 chopped onion
  • ½ cup cream

Steps

  1. Cook the onion until soft.
  2. Add the pumpkin and broth.
  3. Simmer until the pumpkin is tender.
  4. Blend the soup and add the cream.
