Easy Chicken Tacos

Description

Chicken tacos are filled with seasoned chicken, vegetables, and cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound chicken breast
  • 8 small tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • ½ cup shredded cheese

Steps

  1. Cut and cook the chicken.
  2. Warm the tortillas in a pan.
  3. Fill each tortilla with chicken.
  4. Add lettuce and cheese.
