Fluffy Banana Pancakes

Description

Banana pancakes are soft, sweet, and perfect for breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg

Steps

  • Mash the banana in a bowl.
  • Mix in the flour, milk, and egg.
  • Pour small amounts into a hot pan.
  • Cook both sides until golden.
